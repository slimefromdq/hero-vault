# September 15 combat and item rework

This revision supersedes the earlier kit and equipment sections of the development handoff. The current user request overrides the source conversation's suggestion that Oddity protect Irene.

Source material: the supplied item screenshot and the latest full hero table in the accessible **Design Hero Characters** conversation, read through the app. The supplied shared URL could not be fetched directly. The table covers Hazmat, Irene, Oddity, Mexai and Eleanor; Colony and Atlas retain their established scaling identities with real melee attacks.

| Hero | Basic | Signature | Ultimate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hazmat | Slow heavy melee with stagger | Red Gas follows him, damages enemies and reduces healing by 20% | OVERPRESSURE: larger gas, bonus Resolve, healing from gas damage; 90s timer |

| Eleanor | Slow, broad greatsword arc | Intercede: rush to wounded ally, shield and knock enemies back | Hold the Line: 8s of +100 Armor/Resolve, 35% ally damage interception, 80% movement penalty; 100s timer |

Oddity has no Irene following, shielding, rescue relocation or damage-interception behavior, including when copying protective effects. Intermission rearranges enemies without an ally rescue priority. It freezes all combat actions and projectiles, and prevents damage until combat resumes.

## Equipment

Nine items replace the old shop. Two distinct slots per hero and the shared 18-point budget remain. The default squad costs 17 points. Descriptions in the builder expose the current tuning.

| Item | Cost | Prototype tuning |
|---|---:|---|
| Last Stand Shield | 2 | Below 25% HP, 35%-max-HP shield for 5s, once per life. Does not revive lethal hits. |
| Execution Blade | 3 | +60% damage against enemy heroes already below 20% HP. |
| First Hit Hammer | 2 | +60 first basic-hit damage per enemy hero; refresh after 8s without damage exchanged with that hero. |
| Revenge Armor | 2 | +40 Armor/Resolve against a hero who killed the holder, until the holder kills them. |
| Kill Streak Crown | 3 | +8 Power per consecutive hero kill; reset on death. |
| Coward's Boots | 1 | +50% movement below 30% HP while moving away from the nearest visible enemy hero within 240 units. |
| Bodyguard Vest | 2 | 25% damage reduction near a living allied hero with lower current HP, within 120 units. |
| Glass Cannon | 2 | +25 Power, -25 Armor/Resolve; negative defenses amplify incoming damage. |
| Lucky Coin | 1 | Seeded 8% triple-damage chance on a connected basic attack; one roll per melee swing. |

**Double Damage Idol** replaces the Blood Crown's display name and remains a world object: first appears in the jungle at 2:00, doubles carrier damage, drops at the death location, and can be claimed by either team. It is never offered in the loadout catalog. Death drops can occur outside the jungle. Mexai steals equipped items, not the world Idol. Internal `crown_*` event IDs remain stable for existing CSV consumers.

Temporary theft restores the item on expiry or either participant's death. No permanent inventory changes or budget changes occur. Temporary maximum HP is also restored on expiry/death, without resurrecting anyone. Retired saved equipment migrates to empty slots while retaining the saved roster and account progression.

## Map and display

- Horizontal lane span: 700 to 490 world units (30% narrower); vertical span remains 340.
- Base hero radius: 28 to 17 (39% smaller). Colony grows from 17 to 38 by level 13.
- Uniform rendering scale avoids stretching. Two equal-length outer lanes, four towers, two jungle camps and opposite-corner vaults remain.
- Updated melee weapons, red gas fields, buff rings, frozen-combat banner and theft indicators.

## Validation

Godot 4.7.2 headless checks cover item triggers and limits, seed reproducibility, melee misses, item return, HP restoration, multi-target theft, shielding/interception, copying and Intermission. Existing map, duel, jungle, handoff, UI, replay and full-match tests were updated for the new specification. Nine seeded full matches completed by vault destruction; initial runs ended at 264–492 simulated seconds. Native OpenGL screenshots were inspected for the battlefield and loadout UI.

The numerical values here are explicit prototype tuning where the reference gives relative terms such as “large” or “small chance.” This is not a claim of competitive balance. Online play, authored animation, generalized Stability/CC resistance, and advanced projectile/AI tuning remain future work.

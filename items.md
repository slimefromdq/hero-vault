# Items

[Overview](./) · [Heroes](heroes.html) · [Systems](systems.html) · [Items](items.html) · [Roadmap](roadmap.html) · [Agent Handoff](agent-handoff.html)

An item earns its place when it does at least one of these:

1. asks an interesting statistical question;
2. creates a fun visible event.

## Good item behavior

Prefer items that create:
- conditional power;
- clear tradeoffs;
- possession histories;
- transfer/drop drama;
- measurable before/after effects;
- “was it worth it?” arguments.

Avoid piles of tiny invisible percentage bonuses.

## Suggested telemetry

Where relevant, track:
- pickups and drops;
- transfers;
- time held;
- activations;
- damage/healing attributable to the item;
- kills/deaths while held;
- objective interactions.

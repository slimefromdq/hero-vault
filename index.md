# HERO//VAULT

[Overview](./) · [Heroes](heroes.html) · [Systems](systems.html) · [Items](items.html) · [Roadmap](roadmap.html) · [Agent Handoff](agent-handoff.html)

**HERO//VAULT** is a viewing-first hero simulation game built around distinctive characters, uncertain outcomes, readable combat stories, and statistics worth arguing about afterward.

## Design pillars

- **Character fantasy first.** A hero should feel unmistakably like themselves.
- **Fewer, stronger mechanics.** Avoid bloated kits and redundant buttons.
- **Watchability matters.** Systems should create visible drama, not just hidden math.
- **Controlled uncertainty.** Misses, timing, targeting, and randomness keep matches unsolved.
- **Statistics tell stories.** Items and systems should generate interesting questions after the match.
- **Readable complexity.** Spectators should be able to understand why something happened.

## Current rules of thumb

- Heroes progress through **13 levels**; ten heroes are playable.
- Ultimates use **long timers/cooldowns**, not a charge meter.
- Projectiles and attacks can miss when simulation conditions make that plausible.
- Players primarily watch heroes perform rather than manually executing each ability.
- Items should ask an interesting statistical question or create something entertaining to watch.
